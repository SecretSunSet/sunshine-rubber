About the company

Three decades of molded and extruded rubber for Philippine infrastructure.

Sunshine Rubber Tech Resources Co. was incorporated on February 14, 1991 with an initial authorized capitalization of ₱100,000. Actual operations began in May 1992 in Dalandanan, Valenzuela City, with an initial capacity of two metric tons of raw rubber and chemicals per month for the production of high-quality molded and extruded rubber products.

Annual output grew to over a thousand different molded and extruded rubber products — gaskets, steering covers, bushings, laboratory stoppers, furniture footings, impact rollers, door and window strips, port bumpers, expansion joints and bearing pads. By 1994, capitalization was increased to ₱5 million to add high-end machinery.
